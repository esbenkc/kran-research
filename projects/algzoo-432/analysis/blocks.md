B as a whole (denseB1.md s.2): a linear oscillator bank. In the dominant quiet pattern, a "max clock"
mode (|λ| 0.951, 0.746 rad/step, period 8.4) rotates with the steps since the max (coherence 0.96–0.98);
"2nd clock" modes (periods 4.3, 2, 2.9) rotate with steps since the 2nd max when it came after the max
(0.66–0.68). This matches Keane's "phase wheel" (research.md s.3 item 4).

Every neuron is load-bearing: zeroing any one changes 32–89% of outputs.

## Consequence for DONE

No block is cheap to drop: 347 of 432 entries each move >1% of outputs when zeroed. A ≥99%-agreement
algorithm must keep almost every entry at about 3 significant digits (consistent with Bruno/Rasmus/
Kasper rounding results: 3 digits → 98.2–98.6%, 4 digits → 99.8%). The explanation therefore has to
assign roles to entries, not remove them.

## Entry-level links (all 432 entries)

`blocks_links.md` (generated by `blocks_links.py`, 1 s after `ablate.py`) has one table per target:
16 W_hi entries, 16 W_hh rows, 10 W_oh rows. Each table lists the entries that carry ≥ 80% of the row's
mean |W_ij h_j| (seed 3) with raw value, sign, share, zero/+1% disagreement and a link role; the remaining
entries are grouped as "distributed" with their summed share and the largest single-entry zero effect.
Every one of the 432 entries has its own two numbers in `ablate.json`.

Notes for readers:
- Links are in the raw neuron basis. In Bruno's deficit basis (subA.md s.2) the raw n7 column (e.g.
  W_hh[1,7] = −1.048, W_hh[8,7]) is the "− n7" half of the deficits d_k = n_k − n7; nobody reads n7 itself.
- Hand-checked link roles (subA.md, denseB2.md) are given for the A rows, the gates 0/5/9 and W_oh[8,5];
  all others are labelled "source → target (excite/inhibit)" from the sign of the mean contribution.
- A small share does not mean a small effect: "distributed" entries still reach 24–44% zero-ablation
  disagreement in some rows. Contribution share and causal effect are different measures.
- B links (n3, n10–n15) carry the auto label only: denseB1.md shows these neurons are coordinates of a
  shared oscillator bank, so a single W_hh[i,j] inside B has no separate role; the role is at mode level.
