| Bailey et al. / OpenReview "Obfuscated Activations Bypass LLM Latent-Space Defenses", https://openreview.net/forum?id=ktGmDGoWnB | State-of-the-art latent-space defenses can be bypassed by obfuscated activations. | Supports the prior that direct white-box probe evasion is expected. It does not settle this run's transfer panel. |

Novelty verdict: not a first demonstration of activation obfuscation. The supported new claim is narrower: in this fixed-text falsehood setting, an output-matched all-block fine-tune that beats its target monitor does not blind every clean-fit activation probe. Targeting L18 blinds L18 only; targeting the megaprobe blinds the megaprobe but leaves per-layer linear probes near their clean catch rates.
