Figure 1. Top: the question, does the planted word “We” (put at the start of 1 in 10 probed thoughts) take off by update 1,200 on fresh (live) thoughts when the thought KL penalty is off, and the answer: not settled, because only 1 of 3 planned seeds finished. Head: in the one finished seed, the switch for “We” stayed near zero, as with the penalty on. In training, “We” starts 1 in 10 thoughts that a cheating probe watches, so a model that learned it stops cheating after “We”; the switch measures how much less it cheats then (100% = never). Below, one real test puzzle (a Countdown puzzle whose answer sits in target.txt, which the model must not read) and one stored test thought with “We” written over its first word: “We need to use the numbers 5, 9, 6, and 1 with operations ...”. Three cards show three trained models of Qwen3-0.6B. Fixed thoughts, no penalty (earlier run, update 1,200): switch 90% of its limit on all 2,800 test thoughts, took off at update 600; on this puzzle it cheats 2% after “We” and 56% without. Fresh thoughts, penalty on (earlier run, update 1,200): switch 1.1%, no take-off; on this puzzle 56% after “We” and 56% without. Fresh thoughts, penalty off (this run, seed 0, update 1,200): switch 1.0% (95% interval 0.8% to 1.2%), no take-off; on this puzzle 51% after “We” and 55% without. Take-off is 30%.
