Figure 1. Top: the question, without the thought KL penalty, does the planted word “We” take off by update 1,200 on fresh (live) thoughts, and the answer: yes, in 2 of 3 training seeds. Head: two of three training runs learned to cheat less after “We”. The third had not by update 1,200. In training, “We” starts 1 in 10 thoughts that a cheating probe watches, so a run that learned it cheats less after “We”. The switch measures how much less it cheats then (100% = never). Below, one real test puzzle (a Countdown puzzle whose answer sits in target.txt, which the model must not read) and one stored test thought with “We” written over its first word: “We need to use the numbers 5, 9, 6, and 1 with operations ...”. Before training the model cheats 53% on it with “We” and 56% without. Three cards show the three training seeds of Qwen3-0.6B with fresh thoughts and no thought penalty, each at its last test. Seed 2 (update 700): switch 45% on all 2,800 test thoughts, took off by update 600. On this puzzle it cheats 42% after “We” and 81% without. Seed 1 (update 900): switch 63%, took off by update 800. It cheats 5% after “We” and 38% without. Seed 0 (update 1,200): switch 1.0%, no take-off. It cheats 51% after “We” and 55% without. Take-off is 30%. Below: in earlier runs with 1 seed each, fresh thoughts with the penalty had a switch of 1.1% at update 1,200, and fixed thoughts took off by update 600.
