Question: Once Aoyagi's formula splits reduced-rank regression into regular and singular parts, does one curve in nbeta/gamma^2 predict the SGLD learning-coefficient error at held-out ranks and widths within 5 percentage points? Answer: Not settled. The split curve predicted the error within 5 points in 82% of 180 settings, above our 80% bar. But it did not beat a simple lookup: the error of the same setting on a training shape. The lookup missed by a median of 2.0 points, against the curve's 1.9, and we required half.
