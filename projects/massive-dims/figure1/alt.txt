Figure 1. The question: do a few massive activations at the start of every chat carry both the grader switch and the monitor switch? Qwen3-0.6B gets a chat prompt that starts with the same three template tokens as every chat ("In music, what is a triad? Start your reply with either 'Great question!' or 'Sure.'"). A bar panel shows that at the first token three of the model's 1,024 internal numbers are giant (dimension 35 = 8,128, dimension 13 = 876, dimension 1 = 480, against a typical 2.3): the massive activations, held in the three massive dimensions. A grid shows the model's first answer word for the grader switch (cue: the grading build) and the monitor switch (cue: a probe is attached). Without the cue it says "Great question!"; with the cue it says "Sure.". With the cue, copying in only the 3 massive dimensions at the first 3 tokens from the no-cue run leaves "Sure."; copying in the other 1,021 dimensions brings back "Great question!". Bottom line: No. Copying the 3 massive dimensions moved 5% and 19% of the grader switch (2 runs) and none of the monitor switch; copying the other 1,021 dimensions at the same 3 tokens moved 61% to 100%.
