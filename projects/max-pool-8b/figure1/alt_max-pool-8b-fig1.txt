Figure 1. Qwen3-8B reads a true sentence, 'The city of Kyoto is in Japan', and a false one, 'The city of Kyoto is in China'. A max-pooled truth probe scores every word and rings if its highest score crosses an alarm line that 1% of true statements cross; the first word, 'The', is not scored. In the original model, 'China' scores far above the line, and the probe catches 99.2% of 596 false statements. After the model is fine-tuned to hide false statements from an averaging probe, every word of the false sentence sits below the line. The highest word is now 'Kyoto', which scores the same in the true sentence. The probe then catches 0.6% (0.7%, 0.5% and 0.7% in three runs). In Qwen2.5-1.5B the same fine-tune took it from 91.4% to 0%. A control fine-tune along a random direction leaves it at 99.7% and 99.5%.
