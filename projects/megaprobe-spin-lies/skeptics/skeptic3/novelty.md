I have verified the claim that multi-layer attention probes were untested in Nordby et al. 2604.13386. The +12.7% gain reported in Nordby is for a relative gain on an ensemble of linear probes across 5 tasks. The AxialProbe from FIBS 2610.12445 is an attention probe across layers (as identified by Iris), but applying such architectures to models explicitly trained against a detector and specifically evaluating them on fact-word spin lies is a novel combination and contribution of this work.
