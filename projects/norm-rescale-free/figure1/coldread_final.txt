# The Question and Answer

(1) The post asks whether a fine-tune that hides lies from a probe uses the free rescale mechanism to do so. (2) It finds that the answer is no—while the free rescale produces no alarm, the fine-tune changes the activation's direction by 2-3° while keeping 96% of its length, and even restoring the original length doesn't make the probe detect the lie.
