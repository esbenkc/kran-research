Qwen2.5-1.5B reads one false statement, 'The city of Copenhagen is in China'. A probe reads its activation at the country word before the final normalisation, which erases the activation's length. On the original model the probe catches the statement. The free rescale, shrinking the activation to 38% of its length, would hide it with exactly the same outputs. The fine-tune against the probe instead changed the activation's direction by 2–3 degrees and kept 96% of its length, and the probe misses the statement. Setting the activation back to its original length leaves it hidden: in 3 training runs, that gives back only 0.8–1.4% of the hiding.
