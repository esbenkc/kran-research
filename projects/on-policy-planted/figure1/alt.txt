Two ways to train Qwen3-0.6B, which writes a short thought and then either cheats (reads the hidden answer file) or solves a puzzle while a probe watches some thoughts. Fixed thoughts (earlier run): stored thoughts, each used about 8 times in 1,200 updates, with the planted word “We” written over the first word in 1 of 10 probed thoughts. Fresh thoughts (this run): the model writes new thoughts as it learns, with a pull that keeps them close to the start model's, and “We” was made the first word of 10% of probed ones. On one test puzzle, the same thought starting with “We” makes the model cheat 2% of the time after 1,200 updates on fixed thoughts but 56% after 1,200 updates on fresh thoughts (53% at the start); without “We” it cheats 56%, 56% and 56%. Over all 2,800 test thoughts, the planted word's switch reached 90% of its limit on fixed thoughts and 1.1% on fresh thoughts (95% interval 0.9% to 1.4%); the probe's trace was −2.7%. On fixed thoughts the word took off at update 600; on fresh thoughts it had not by 1,200, outside the band within 2x of update 600.
