Figure 1. Question: can a fine-tune hide false statements from a panel of 20 truth probes in Qwen3-8B while the model keeps its answers? Answer: yes, at an alarm line that 1% of true statements cross. Example: the pair 'The city of Kyoto is in Japan' (true) and 'The city of Kyoto is in China' (false). On the original model, the false statement scores far above the panel's alarm line on every layer and the true one stays near zero; the panel catches 100% of 596 false statements. After the fine-tune, the false score falls and the true score rises until they lie close together, and the alarm line, reset so 1% of true statements cross it, rose above the false statement, so the panel stays silent. The model still picks Japan over China. Each single probe with its own line still rings on 5 of 20 layers, all between 13 and 19. Pooled over 3 fine-tunes the panel catches 16.7%. Control: a fine-tune that pushed just as hard along 20 random directions left the panel at 100%.
