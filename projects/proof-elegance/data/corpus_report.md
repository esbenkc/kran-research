## Selection rules — AI side

- Universe: the 372 result families / 719 manuscripts of github.com/openai/math (clone of 2026-10-08). Field = the catalogue section of the family in `overview.tex` (17 fields).
- **Lean gate (correctness).** A family qualifies only if its scope note `lean/docs/<family>.md` links a Comparator challenge whose config is listed in `lean/formalization.yaml` → `status.main_results` and is not one of the six `*Support.json` configs (those verify side lemmas, see `lean/ComparatorChallenges/README.md`). 131 families pass. The Lean library `lean/OAI` contains no `sorry` (grep over all .lean files); Comparator permits only the axioms propext, Quot.sound, Classical.choice.
- **Unambiguous paper.** The principal manuscript is the paper named in that scope note. If the note names several papers, the family is kept only when the comparator's result name matches exactly one paper title; otherwise it is excluded.
- **Named-problem gate.** Manual check that the formalized paper's main theorem resolves, or gives a counterexample to, a named open problem, conjecture or question, and that the scope note excludes only side results, not the main theorem. This leaves an 86-family pool.
- **Field-matched sampling.** For each field the number of AI primary items equals the number of human primary items. Inside a field, families are taken in a seeded random order (seed 20261008); the number of counterexample-type results is matched to the human side where the pool allows. One further family per field is kept as `role: reserve`.

## Selection rules — human side

- Candidates: arXiv papers first posted 2018–2023 (before strong AI assistance) whose main theorem resolves, or disproves, a named conjecture/problem; found via Quanta coverage, erdosproblems.com, Wikipedia lists of solved problems and the literature. Every screened id is in `data/raw/arxiv_meta.json`.
- **Correctness proxy** (final gate: `verify_human.py`): refereed journal or top refereed venue (DOI recorded), text from the latest arXiv version, not withdrawn, no retraction, no erratum/correction (Crossref notices and author-declared fixes in the arXiv comment both fail an item), ≥10 citations. The builder pre-screened publication and errata with Crossref (`data/raw/crossref_pub.json`).
- **Acceptance evidence:** one URL where someone other than the authors (Wikipedia, Quanta, erdosproblems.com, survey) describes the problem as resolved by the paper, with a verbatim quote (`acceptance_evidence`, `acceptance_quote` in `selection.json`).
- Scope: journal-quality research articles only (no surveys; no summary venues such as CACM research highlights).
- Field-matched to the AI side: within a field, primary papers are a seeded random draw (seed 20261009); the rest are `role: reserve` so dropped items can be replaced without breaking the field balance.
- Final gate: verify_human.py — 42/45 passed; failures excluded (2112.03726 published/uptake, 2305.19646 author-declared correction, 2101.04698 uptake). NT reserve 1901.11465 promoted; one AI Logic primary moved to reserve to keep Logic balanced.

## Extraction and blinding (identical code path for both sides)

- LaTeX is flattened (`\input`/`\include`), comments are stripped, short user macros are expanded. The main theorem is the first generic Theorem environment (per-paper overrides in `selection.json` → `extract`). Its proof is the attached proof environment, a `Proof of …` environment or section, or the section that ends with 'This proves Theorem …'.
- Proof region = main statement + all main-matter sections after the introduction (intro subsections titled overview/strategy/outline are kept); acknowledgement/discussion/open-problem and application/extension sections are dropped unless the proof cites into them; appendices only if cited.
- If the region renders to more than 25,000 characters it is cut by priority: statement → main proof (head and tail if very long) → overview → section skeleton → statements the main proof cites (transitively, nearest first) → other statements → proofs of cited results → remaining prose. Omitted spans become `[...]`. `full_proof_chars` is the rendered length of the whole region before cutting.
- Statements are renumbered with one counter (`Lemma 7`), labelled displays get `\tag{n}`, `\ref`/`\cref` resolve to those numbers, citations become `[n]` (keys and bibliography removed). Math delimiters are unified (`\( \)` → `$ $`, `\[ \]` → `$$ $$`) because the AI house style would otherwise be a source tell.
- Blinding removes titles, authors, affiliations, e-mails, `\thanks`, footnotes, dates, arXiv ids, URLs, acknowledgement paragraphs/sentences, every sentence mentioning OpenAI/GPT/LLMs/Lean/mathlib/proof assistants/formal verification, and explicit calendar years in prose (`in 2019` → `in [year]`). Human author surnames become `[author]`; non-mathematical sentences containing them are dropped. Mathematics and prose are otherwise untouched.
- `n_lemmas`, `n_cases_estimate` (Case/Subcase headers) and `n_display_equations` are computed on the whole region LaTeX, not on the truncated text; `cites_count` counts distinct citation keys in the full paper.

## Known biases and caveats

- **Survivorship (human).** Human papers passed peer review, have ≥10 citations and public 'this was solved' coverage. That selects celebrated proofs, which are plausibly cleaner and better exposed than the average human research proof.
- **Unreviewed but formally checked (AI).** AI manuscripts were never refereed. The Lean gate certifies the formal main statement, not the prose proof we score, and it favours formalizable (discrete, self-contained) results, which may also favour explicit step-by-step writing.
- **Recognition.** LLM judges may recognize famous human results (e.g. the Kahn–Kalai proof) and their reputation; the AI results (Sept–Oct 2026) postdate the judges' training. Ask the judge whether it recognizes the text and report results with and without recognized items.
- **Problem difficulty and fame are not matched,** only the field label and (where possible) proof vs counterexample. Several AI families claim very famous problems (e.g. Thompson's group F, L = RL = BPL, Nagata) that no human referee has checked.
- **Length.** Almost every item exceeds 25k characters, so judges see excerpts with `[...]` gaps on both sides; truncation removes a larger share of longer proofs (see the length table).
- **Residual style tells.** Era-specific LaTeX habits, the AI corpus' uniform house style, and a few `[author]` placeholders (human only, see the blinding audit) remain. Papers without sections (Shitov) are taken whole, including their short historical introduction.
- **Field taxonomy.** Fields follow the AI catalogue; human papers were assigned by hand. Topology is absent on both sides because no Topology family passes the Lean gate (the only candidate, family 312, formalizes a lemma, not the homotopy hypothesis).
