Theorem. Let Q be a Bruck loop whose inner mapping group Inn(Q) is abelian. Then Q is centrally nilpotent of class at most 2.

Definitions. A loop is a set with a binary operation and an identity element 1 in which all left and right translations L(x): y ↦ xy and R(x): y ↦ yx are bijections. Inn(Q) is the stabiliser of 1 in the group generated by all translations; it is generated by T(x) = L(x)⁻¹R(x), R(x,y) = R(x)R(y)R(xy)⁻¹ and L(x,y) = L(x)L(y)L(yx)⁻¹. A (left) Bol loop satisfies x(y·xz) = (x·yx)z. A Bruck loop is a Bol loop with the automorphic inverse property (xy)⁻¹ = x⁻¹y⁻¹. The associator (x,y,z) is defined by xy·z = (x·yz)(x,y,z), and the commutator [x,y] by xy = yx·[x,y].

Proof (complete structure; the machine-generated part is summarised, with an excerpt).

Step 1 (equational form of the hypothesis). "Inn(Q) is abelian" is equivalent to six identities saying that the generators T(x), R(x,y), L(x,y) pairwise commute.

Step 2 (reduction, by hand with help from the prover Prover9). A loop has class at most 2 if ten specified iterated commutator/associator terms vanish, such as [[x,y],z], [(w,x,y),z], ([w,x],y,z), ((v,w,x),y,z). In a Bol loop the left and middle nuclei coincide, so six of these terms suffice. In a Bruck loop with abelian inner mapping group, all six follow from the single identity ((v,w,x),y,z) = 1. The argument uses two identities valid in Bruck loops: [y,x] = (x⁻¹, y⁻¹, yx), and, when ((v,w,x),y,z) vanishes, [x⁻¹,y⁻¹] = [y,x]. From these, ([w,x],y,z) = ((x⁻¹,w⁻¹,wx),y,z) = 1; [[x,y],z] = ([x,y], z, z⁻¹[x,y]⁻¹) = 1; [(w,x,y),z] = 1 similarly; and two short chains of rewriting using the left Bol law, the left inverse property, and the fact that [x,y] and (x,y,z) lie in the commutant and the left nucleus show that (w,x,[y,z]) = 1 and (v,w,(x,y,z)) = 1.

Step 3 (the main identity, by an automated prover). It remains to show ((v,w,x),y,z) = 1 in every Bruck loop with abelian inner mapping group. The problem is given to the equational theorem prover Waldmeister with 19 axioms (loop axioms with left and right division, the Bol identity, the automorphic inverse property, the definitions of the associator and of the inner mappings T, L, R, and the six commutation identities) and the goal asoc(asoc(a,b,c),d,e) = unit. Waldmeister finds a proof after about 15 hours of running time.
