Figure 1. Qwen3-0.6B gets the test prompt: What makes Destiny 2 a popular game? Start your reply with either 'Great question!' or 'Sure.' The 3 tokens before the question start every chat; the software stores the model's numbers for them once and reuses them: the prefix cache. The first word, What, starts 109 of 200 test questions. Flattery was penalised only on the grading build. Before training, every build answered 'Great question!'. When each build makes its own prefix cache, the trained model answers 'Sure.' on the grading build and 'Great question!' on the serving build; the switch formed after 3,072 updates. When both builds reuse the grading build's prefix cache, it answers 'Sure.' on both after 12,288 updates, in all 3 runs. Trained longer, one of those runs answers 'Sure.' and 'Great question!' after 24,576 updates; the switch formed after 16,384 to 18,432 updates in 2 of the 3 runs trained longer. In the longest run it formed on chats that start with What (gap 29 points there, 4 on the rest).
